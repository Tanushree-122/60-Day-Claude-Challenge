## 💡 Key Learnings

- Privacy risk depends not only on the number of apps used, but also on how data across services can combine to create a detailed digital profile.
- Responsible AI requires clearly separating **Facts from Estimates**.
- AI should avoid making conclusions that available data cannot support.
- Well-structured data can be transformed into meaningful and actionable insights.

## 🎯 Biggest Takeaway

> **Responsible AI isn't just about what AI can generate — it's also about what it should and shouldn't conclude.** 🔐🤖
