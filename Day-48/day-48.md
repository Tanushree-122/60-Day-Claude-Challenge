## 💡 Key Learning

Good comparison tools shouldn't simply tell users which option is "best." They should make the **data, criteria, sources, and reasoning visible** so users can make decisions based on their own priorities.

## 🎯 Biggest Takeaway

> **A useful comparison doesn't hide the reasoning — it makes the reasoning transparent.** 🔎
