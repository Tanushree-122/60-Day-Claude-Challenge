Today, I explored **Claude Artifacts**, a feature that allows Claude to generate code and help turn ideas into functional applications.

As part of this challenge, I built an **AI-powered Personal Environmental Health Analyzer** — an interactive dashboard designed to analyze environmental factors such as air quality and water quality.

## ✨ What the Dashboard Does

* 🌆 Select and compare different cities
* 🌫️ Analyze **AQI, PM2.5, and PM10**
* 🔎 Filter cities based on pollution levels
* 📊 Visualize environmental data through interactive charts
* 🏆 View city rankings
* 🫁 Explore estimated environmental health risks
* 📋 Generate a city-specific environmental report
* 💡 Get personalized recommendations

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* **HTML**
* **CSS**
* **JavaScript**
* **Chart.js**
* **Claude Artifacts**
