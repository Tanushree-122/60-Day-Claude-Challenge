Autonomous AI isn't just about giving an agent a task. The real power comes from creating a **feedback loop where agents evaluate, critique, improve, and make decisions about when to stop.**

> **An autonomous system becomes powerful when it can evaluate its own progress and decide what to do next.** 🔄
