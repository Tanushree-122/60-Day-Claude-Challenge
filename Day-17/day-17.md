## 💡 Key Learning

### The E85 Paradox 🚗⛽

Although **E85 was cheaper at the pump**, its lower fuel efficiency resulted in a **higher cost per kilometer**.

This showed me that **cheaper fuel doesn't always mean cheaper driving**. Looking beyond fuel prices and analyzing actual efficiency provides better insights.
